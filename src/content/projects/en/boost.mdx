## The problem

People who train and watch what they eat usually run two apps that never talk: one for the gym and one for food. The training plan does not know what you ate, and the calorie goal does not know how hard you trained.

## The solution

Boost puts both in one phone-first app. Home shows the calories and macros left today, today's workout and a tip you can accept or dismiss. In the gym you log sets, reps, weight and RPE live, with a rest timer. Routines are built from a catalogue of 1,324 exercises, and meals from a search that joins a local catalogue with Open Food Facts.

## Technical decisions

The AI never decides a number. Rules written in the backend choose each tip, its figures and the action behind the Accept button. The model can only reword the text, and what it writes passes a safety filter before it reaches the screen.

The rules have fixed limits: they never diagnose, never propose a deficit above 500 kcal a day and never raise training load more than 10% a week. The calorie goal comes from the Mifflin-St Jeor formula.

The code keeps API routes, business logic, persistence and frontend calls in separate layers. The Supabase tables are closed with RLS to everything except the backend.
